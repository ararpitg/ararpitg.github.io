Arpit Gaind

Scholar · Practitioner · Educator

Hello, I’m Arpit.

I’m a scholar-practitioner working at the intersection of documentary film, digital humanities, and critical archival studies, centered on Indigenous media sovereignty. Over the past decade I’ve built long-term collaborations with rural and Indigenous communities across Jharkhand and Chhattisgarh, using participatory filmmaking to challenge dominant narratives about Adivasi life. I bring that same commitment to transparency into my teaching and institutional work at UCLA.

Get in Touch

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Culture and Performance at UCLA’s Department of World Arts and Cultures/Dance, School of Arts and Architecture, with more than a decade of research, teaching, and filmmaking experience examining Indigenous media sovereignty and digital archives among Adivasi communities in Jharkhand, India.

As an educator, I serve as UCLA’s Teaching Assistant Consultant (2025–2026), mentoring graduate instructors in pedagogical design, and hold graduate certificates in Digital Humanities and Writing Pedagogy. My teaching spans media production, critical theory, and writing-based seminars. My institutional leadership includes chairing the ASUCLA Communications Board and guest-editing volume 57 of Comitatus.
